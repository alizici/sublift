Sublift

Turns the subtitles burned into a video into an SRT file, exact to the frame.

Getting started

  1. Create a project for a series with New Project (⌘N).
  2. Drop its episodes on the project, or drop a whole folder. Episodes are numbered from their file names.
  3. On a project's first episode, check the subtitle area: drag its top or bottom edge if needed, then choose Read Subtitles. The project's other episodes use the same area.
  4. Episodes are read one after another. Open one to check its subtitles with the keyboard.

Keyboard

A DMove the selected start or end one frame back or forward.
W SSelect the start or the end of the subtitle.
PPlay the moment with the subtitle drawn on top; press again to stop.
ReturnEdit the text. Return keeps it, Esc undoes, Option-Return starts a new line.
SpaceMark the subtitle checked and go to the next one.
⌫Delete the selected subtitles.

Every part of the app has an ⓘ button that explains it, and Help → Sublift Help has everything on one page.

Questions

Which languages does Sublift read?

Chinese. For bilingual subtitles, the translation line can be left out (Settings → Only Chinese lines).

Which videos can I open?

MP4, MKV, MOV, TS and other common files with H.264, HEVC, VP9 or AV1 video. AV1 needs a Mac with M3 or later. Sublift requires a Mac with Apple silicon.

Where are my subtitles saved?

In Sublift's own database, together with your checks and edits; every change is saved by itself. Sublift can also keep an up-to-date SRT next to each video (Settings), and you can export any episode, or a whole project into one folder.

Why does Sublift ask for permission to write in a folder?

macOS lets apps write only where you allow it. Sublift asks once per folder to keep the SRT next to your videos. If you say no, nothing is lost: the subtitles stay in Sublift and you can export them. To allow it later, right-click the episode and choose Allow SRT Next to Video…. Dropping a whole folder gives the permission at once.

I moved my videos. What now?

Sublift usually finds a moved or renamed video by itself. If it can't, right-click the episode and choose Locate Video…. Your subtitles and checks are kept.

Some lines are marked as weak readings.

Two recognition models read every line; when they don't fully agree, the line is marked so you can look at it. Choose Weak at the bottom of the list to see only those.

How can I get the best results?

  • Make the subtitle area cover the subtitle line and not much more; text outside it (signs, titles, credits) is not read.
  • For yellow or other coloured subtitles, turn on Coloured subtitles in Settings.

Is Sublift free?

You can read three episodes for free. After that, a one-time purchase unlocks Sublift for as many episodes and projects as you like. Episodes already read always stay yours. Bought it before? Choose Restore Purchase in the unlock window.

Does Sublift upload my videos?

No. Everything happens on your Mac. The only thing Sublift sends is a feedback message you write. See the privacy policy.
