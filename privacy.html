Privacy Policy

Sublift · effective 5 November 2026

Your videos and subtitles never leave your Mac. The only thing Sublift ever sends is a feedback message you choose to write.

What Sublift does with your files

  • Sublift opens only the videos you add to it (by dropping them, choosing them, or locating a moved video) and writes only where you allow it: the SRT files you export, and the SRT kept next to a video in folders you approve.
  • Videos are decoded and their subtitles recognized on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Projects, subtitles, your edits and checks, and the places of your videos are stored in a database inside Sublift's own container on your Mac. Removing a project or the app removes them; your videos and SRT files are never deleted by Sublift.

Feedback

When you write in Sublift's Feedback window and press Return, Sublift sends to the developer:

  • your message;
  • your email address, only if you enter one so you can get an answer;
  • the Sublift version, the macOS version and the Mac model, to help with bug reports.

The message is delivered by email through Web3Forms and used only to answer you and improve Sublift. It is not linked to your identity unless you add your email, not used for tracking, and not shared or sold. Nothing is sent unless you write and send a message.

What Sublift does not do

  • It does not upload your videos, subtitles or any other file.
  • It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • It does not sell or share any personal information.

Purchases

Sublift can be tried with three free episodes and unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase. Apple handles the purchase and may collect information under its own privacy policy. Crash reports reach the developer only if you choose to share them with app developers in macOS settings; they are anonymous and provided by Apple.

Children

Sublift does not ask for personal information. The only information it can send is a feedback message the user chooses to write, as described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
